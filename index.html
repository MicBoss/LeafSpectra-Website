What is LeafSpectra?

A gentle start for growing vegetables at home.

LeafSpectra is an Android app designed for people who are new to growing vegetables. It brings your growing setup, simple guidance and your plants' history into one place, with Leafity to keep you company.

Your space

Understand your growing space

Describe where you live and how you grow at home, so guidance can fit your conditions.

Vegetables

Learn which vegetables may suit you

See vegetable suggestions matched to your location and home-growing setup.

Guidance

Follow planting and care guidance

Beginner-friendly instructions and care tips for each growth stage, with weather-aware advice and reminders.

Progress

Keep track of plant progress

Each plant keeps its own profile, care records and history, so you can look back on how it has grown.

How it works

From first setup to watching your plants change.

This is the intended experience inside the Android app.

  1. 1

    Set up your growing profile

    Create an account and tell the app about your location and home-growing setup.

  2. 2

    Explore suitable vegetables

    Browse vegetables suggested for your conditions and pick what to grow.

  3. 3

    Learn and record plant care

    Follow stage-by-stage guidance, get reminders, and log the care you give.

  4. 4

    Complete guided observations

    When your plant is eligible, the app guides you through taking suitable leaf photos.

  5. 5

    Review visible changes

    See what has visibly changed over time, with Leafity's cautious explanations.

Visual monitoring needs suitable leaf images and is limited to the supported vegetables and observation steps. Its results describe visible changes — they are not confirmed diagnoses.

FAQ

Questions beginners often ask.

What is LeafSpectra?

LeafSpectra is an online planting and plant-care assistant with visual monitoring. It helps you choose vegetables, follow beginner-friendly growing guidance, keep care records, and look at how your plants' leaves visibly change over time.

Who is the app for?

It is designed for novice home vegetable growers — people growing a few vegetables at home who want simple, step-by-step help.

Is it available on Android?

Yes, LeafSpectra's main product is an Android app. It will be offered here as an official APK installer once the release is ready.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. The app's main functions run on online services, so an internet connection is required for accounts, recommendations, weather-aware advice, leaf-image analysis and Leafity.

Does LeafSpectra diagnose plant diseases?

No. LeafSpectra describes visible leaf changes from guided photos for the vegetables it supports. It does not give confirmed disease diagnoses, does not identify pests automatically, and does not prescribe chemical treatments. It also does not use soil-moisture sensors or automatic watering equipment.

How does Leafity help?

Leafity is a conversational plant-care companion inside the app, in English and Filipino. Leafity explains planting information and care guidance in simple words and points you to your next small step.

How do I download and install the app?

When the installer is released, use the Download button on this page to get the APK on your Android phone, open the file, allow the installation if Android asks, and follow the steps on screen.
